MY AUTOBIOGRAPHY

My Life in High School

My high school days were fun and busy.
I studied Technical Drafting and Animation. I learned to draw plans, make 3D models, and create short animations on the computer.
Sometimes it was hard work, but I liked making new things.

Working with Friends

I made many good friends in class.
We helped each other with computer work and school projects. We talked, laughed, and worked together every day.
I am very happy for my time in high school.

Things We Did in Class

  • Drawing house plans and 3D shapes
  • Making characters and simple animations
  • Working together on school projects
  • Having fun with friends after class
What I Learned
  1. Be careful with every detail in my drawings.
  2. Be patient when working on the computer.
  3. Save my work often so I do not lose it.
